Programme by day

  1. Electricity and measurements Voltage, current, resistance, multimeter — measuring without mistakes.
    • Ohm's law with examples from a car
    • Multimeter: voltage, current, resistance, continuity test
    • Voltage drop under load: why "12 volts are there" but it still does not work
    • Finding a parasitic current drain on a parked car
  2. Wiring diagrams and fault finding How to read diagrams and build diagnostic logic instead of replacing parts at random.
    • Symbols on factory diagrams, connector pins, wire colours
    • Relays, fuses, grounds and connectors
    • Open circuit, short circuit, poor contact — step-by-step fault tracing
    • Diagnostic plan: from the customer's complaint to the cause
  3. Sensors and actuators Supply, signal, ground. Testing on the bench and on the car.
    • Position sensors: crankshaft, camshaft, throttle, accelerator pedal
    • Temperature, pressure, MAP / MAF, lambda sensor
    • Injectors, coils, valves, small motors — how to test them
    • Telling a faulty sensor from faulty wiring
  4. Oscilloscope in diagnostics Live signals: when a scanner is not enough and what to look at on the screen.
    • Setting up for a car: scale, timebase, triggering
    • Crankshaft, camshaft, injector and coil signals
    • CAN and LIN buses: what a healthy and a faulty bus look like
    • When the scanner shows nothing but the oscilloscope shows the cause
  5. Diagnostics with Launch Working with the Launch scanner: codes, data, service functions, real cases.
    • Reading and interpreting fault codes, freeze frames
    • Live data: which values are normal and which are not
    • Service functions: adaptations, resets, learning procedures
    • Scanner and oscilloscope together on the same fault
  6. Reading and writing firmware OBD / Bench / Boot — which method to use when, and how not to damage the unit.
    • OBD, Bench and Boot methods: when to choose which
    • Connecting to the unit and keeping the power supply stable during the job
    • A backup before any write
    • Typical mistakes after which a unit stops responding, and how to avoid them
  7. Chip tuning in practice What is adjusted, the stages of the work, checks after flashing.
    • Which parameters are adjusted and why
    • Stages: read → backup → edit → write → verify
    • Checks after flashing: fault codes and live data
    • Where the limit of safe tuning lies
  8. Protected ECUs How to work with units protected by the manufacturer — in practice.
    • Why a unit is protected and how to recognise it
    • Switching to Bench / Boot when the unit does not allow access over OBD
    • Practice on real units
  9. Immobilizer — theory How the system is built and how to diagnose it through the control unit.
    • How an immobilizer is built: key, antenna, units (theory)
    • Immobilizer diagnostics through the control unit (ECU)
    • Key making and key programming are not covered
  10. Choosing and buying equipment What you really need to start, where you can save money and where you should not.
    • The minimum set to get started
    • Scanners, oscilloscopes, programmers: what to buy
    • Where you can save and where saving costs more in the end
  11. A technician's workflow Vehicle intake, diagnostic order, talking to the customer.
    • Vehicle intake and questioning the customer
    • Diagnostic order and recording the results
    • How to explain the cause and the cost of the repair to the customer
  12. Practice on real cases Real cars and units, going through questions, what to take on after the course.
    • Diagnosing real cars from start to finish
    • Going through the questions that came up during the course
    • What you can take on right after the course

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

Prior knowledge is not required: the course starts with the basics of electricity and measurements. If you already have experience, you will get to the advanced topics faster.

Do I need my own equipment?

Practice is done on a Launch scanner and an oscilloscope. On day 10 we go through what equipment to buy for your own work.

It starts on 7 December at 12:00. 12 class days, Monday to Friday, the last day is 22 December.
